# Credits

Everything here is free to use in this project. Every license page was checked by hand
on 2026-10-19/20. No stock-site previews or watermarked images are used.

## Creature art

The 16 creatures are the project's own art (Genome Wrapped), and the walk cycles come
from the project's own clips.
